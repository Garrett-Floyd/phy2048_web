Theorem 8.3.2.

Let \(\vec{v}_{BA}\) be the velocity of observer \(B\) in observer \(A\)’s reference frame. Let \(\vec{v}_{CB}\) be the velocity of the object in observer \(B\)’s reference frame. Let \(\vec{v}_{CA}\) be the velocity of the object in observer \(A\)’s reference frame. Then
\begin{equation*} \vec{v}_{CB} = \vec{v}_{CA} - \vec{v}_{BA}. \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

Proof.

From Figure 8.2.1 we see that
\begin{equation*} \vec{r}_{CB} + \vec{r}_{BA} = \vec{r}_{CA}. \end{equation*}
Then
\begin{align*} \frac{d}{dt}(\vec{r}_{CB} + \vec{r}_{BA}) \amp = \frac{d\vec{r}_{CA}}{dt} \\ \\ \vec{v}_{CB} + \vec{v}_{BA} \amp = \vec{v}_{CA} \\ \\ \vec{v}_{CB} \amp = \vec{v}_{CA} - \vec{v}_{BA} \end{align*}
